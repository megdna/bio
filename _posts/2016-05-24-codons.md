<h3><em>Coding for Life</em></h3>

<a href="https://github.com/codons" target="_blank">Codons</a> is a network that aims to connect genomic data and medical information collected from diagnostic devices and health monitoring sensors with machine learning algorithms in the fog, allowing for local computation on-site.
